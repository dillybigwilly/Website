Our services

A look at jobs we can handle across Ottawa & surrounding areas. Real photos coming as we keep adding them…

Junk removal

Junk & estate cleanouts

Whether it’s a single item on the curb, a basement of forty years, or a full estate, we handle the lift and the haul. We sort as we go — recyclables to the right facility, donatable goods to local shelters, and only what’s left to the transfer station. Same-day pickup when the schedule allows.

  • Single-item pickups to full-property cleanouts
  • 5-ton dump trailer for big loads, light truck for small ones
  • Sort, recycle and donate first — landfill last
Demolition & site prep

Demolition, tear-out & site prep

Residential and commercial work — sheds, garages, decks, pool surrounds, interior strip-outs and small outbuildings. We also handle larger lot clearance and site prep: pulling multiple outbuildings, hauling mixed debris, grading the dirt flat with the mini-ex. Lumber sorted for recycling, concrete and metal to the right place, site raked smooth for the next trade.

  • Residential & commercial projects
  • Structures up to roughly 1,000 sq ft
  • Hand-off ready for the next trade on site
Lawn care

Lawn & property care

The recurring work your weekend keeps eating. Weekly mowing routes across Ottawa, edging, hedge trimming, mulch and bed refresh, eavestrough clearing. Spring and fall cleanups when the seasons turn. Lock in a regular slot or call us for a one-shot job — your choice.

  • Weekly, biweekly or one-time mowing & edging
  • Spring & fall property cleanups, mulch refresh
  • Hedge trimming, bed work, eavestrough clearing
Tree removal

Tree removal & pruning

Simple tree take-downs, limb work and pruning, stump grinding flush to grade, and storm response when something’s come down where it shouldn’t be. Split firewood stacked for the homeowner on request — otherwise chips and brush hauled off-site.

  • Simple residential tree take-downs, tight access OK
  • Stump grinding flush to grade, full chip-and-haul
  • Storm response priority — call, don’t email
Window & glass

Window hardware & glass repair

The fixes that keep your windows working — without replacing the whole unit. Cranks that won’t turn, latches that won’t catch, hinges, locks, weather stripping, broken panes and torn screens. We source the right hardware, install it clean, and check the seal so the next storm doesn’t make it inside.

  • Crank, latch, hinge & lock replacement
  • Weather stripping & seal renewal
  • Single-pane glass, screen patch & rebuild
Handyman

Handyman work

The list of small fixes you keep meaning to get to. Doors that won’t latch, drywall patches, fixture and faucet swaps, garage shelving, blinds, mounting, furniture assembly, hauling things upstairs. Tools live on the truck. One visit, one invoice — no nickel-and-diming.

  • Half-day & full-day punch-list pricing
  • Drywall, doors, fixtures, mounting, assembly, hauling
  • One crew, one trip, one invoice
